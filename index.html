Honker Band from Binghamton

Bio

Honker is a high-energy funk-rock jam band from Binghamton, NY. The three of us write and play groove-heavy originals with catchy hooks, solid improvisation, and a whole lot of feel-good energy. Our shows are sweaty, danceable, and always a little different — we like to stretch out and let the music go where it wants.
Influenced by bands like moe., Phish, Zappa, and Rush, we mix tight songs with exploratory jams. You can catch us at local spots like Atomic Tom’s, Abel’s Pub, and Studio 56, plus the occasional festival.
Current lineup:
Jim Lomonaco (Yolk, Caviar & Grits) – Bass & Vocals
Nate Calzetoni (Grateful Upstate Toodleloo) – Guitar & Vocals
Jeff Howard (Randy McStine Trio, Yolk, Grateful Upstate Toodleloo) – Drums & Vocals
